# About Me

## Family Life

I grew up near Columbus, Ohio. When I was a teenager, my family moved to Jackson, Ohio, where we lived for about 10 years. Afterward, I moved back to the Columbus area.

After getting married, I relocated to the Seattle area to pursue a career as a Software Engineer in the big tech industry. My wife and I share a passion for hiking, and the beautiful trails in this region have provided us with countless unique experiences. We have a daughter, and we make it a tradition to visit our family every year — at least once the pandemic was over and air travel was not limited.
